# Launch gates

The repository now contains the production service and app integration. The former demo-only architecture is retained for local reference, not deployed.

See [deployment and operations](deployment.md) for required hosting, verified email sender, private backup bucket, origin and moderator/support setup, and [readiness](readiness.md) for completed checks and remaining external validation.

Do not invite real students until real-inbox sign-in, HTTPS deployment, first offsite backup plus recovery, moderation operations, native device permissions, and two-account privacy/revocation have been checked on the actual deployment. Signed native builds and store submission require the operator's accounts and credentials.
